FORGE

An offline workout tracker for Android

Plan your week, log every set in seconds, and watch your strength grow.
  • No ads, ever.
  • Your data is yours: backup, restore, export and import are free forever.
  • Nothing free today becomes paid later.

Features

  • Weekly plans, supersets and warm-up sets
  • Rest timer on the lock screen
  • Personal records, progression hints and per-exercise charts
  • Training year heatmap, streaks and achievements
  • Bodyweight and body measurements
  • Backup, CSV export, and import from FitNotes or Hevy

Privacy

FORGE has no account and does not use the internet. Everything stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
