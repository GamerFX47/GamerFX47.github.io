FORGE Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Applies to the FORGE app for Android (io.github.gamerfx47.forge)

In short
  • FORGE has no account and does not connect to the internet.
  • Everything you log stays on your phone. Nothing is collected, sold or shared.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Backups and exports are files that you save and share yourself.

Who we are

FORGE is a workout tracker made by Kindstead, an independent developer. Questions about this policy or your data: support@kindstead.dev.

Information FORGE stores

To work as a training log, FORGE stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Workouts, sets, weights, reps, notes and workout durations
  • Your plans, exercises and templates
  • Bodyweight, body fat and body measurements, if you choose to log them
  • App settings (theme, rest timer, progression hints) and the date you became a Founding Member

This information never leaves your phone unless you export or share it yourself. The developer cannot see it.

Information FORGE does not collect

FORGE does not request internet access. It does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising IDs, and it contains no advertising or analytics software from other companies.

Health and fitness information

Workouts and body measurements are fitness information. FORGE keeps them on your device and never sells them, shares them, uses them for advertising, or uses them for anything other than showing your own history and progress to you.

FORGE is a training log, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice.

Permissions

NotificationsShows the rest timer countdown on the lock screen and alerts you when rest ends.
VibrationVibrates when rest ends.
Alarms & remindersOptional. Lets the end-of-rest alert ring on time while the phone is asleep. You can allow or remove it in Android settings.

FORGE does not ask for access to your photos, files, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Backups, exports and imports

When you back up, export a spreadsheet (CSV), or import data from FitNotes or Hevy, you choose the file with Android's own file picker or share sheet. FORGE reads or writes only the file you pick. Where you save or send a backup (for example a cloud drive, email or a computer) is up to you, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual workouts and entries in the app. To delete everything, clear FORGE's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children

FORGE is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Future paid features

FORGE may later offer optional paid features through Google Play. Payments would be handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; FORGE would only learn whether a purchase is active. This policy will be updated before any such change, and the update date above will change.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
